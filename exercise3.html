Scenario

Meteorological stations transmit compact telemetry strings containing station IDs, dates, temperatures in Celsius and humidity percentages, including occasional sensor errors (ERR). Your task is to parse these codes, impute missing data and convert units with NumPy, compute a heat discomfort index in Pandas and generate a weather alert bulletin file.

  1. Parse stringsSplit, clean, flag ERR as NaN9 strings → 4 lists
  2. NumPyImpute NaN, °C → °F, alert flagslists → arrays
  3. PandasDataFrame, heat index, sortingarrays → df
  4. ReportGroupBy summary, CSV, bulletindf → 2 files
